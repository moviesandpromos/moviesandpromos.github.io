Understanding Your Results

A backtest tells you how a strategy would have performed on past data. It does not predict the future. Focus on understanding how the money was made — not just whether it was made.

Core Metrics

Total Return (%)

Percent growth of your capital over the test.

₹1,00,000 → ₹1,18,000 = +18%
Tip

Compare with buy-and-hold to see if the strategy added value.

Final Capital (₹)

Value at the end of the test, including open positions.

₹1,18,420

CAGR (%)

Annualised return — the yearly equivalent.

18% over 2 years ≈ 8.6% CAGR
Tip

Very short tests (under 30 days) can produce misleading CAGR.

Total Trades

Number of completed buy-sell round trips.

24 trades

Win Rate (%)

Percentage of trades that were profitable.

62.5% means 15 of 24 trades made money
Tip

A high win rate alone does not mean profit — check Profit Factor too.

Profit Factor

Gross profits divided by gross losses.

1.84 means ₹1.84 earned for every ₹1 lost
Tip

Above 1.5 is generally healthy. Above 2 is very good.

Maximum Drawdown (%)

Largest fall from a peak before recovery.

12% means your portfolio temporarily fell from ₹1,10,000 to ₹96,800
Tip

Lower is easier to tolerate emotionally.

Net Profit After Costs (₹)

Profit after transaction costs, slippage, and dividends.

₹18,420

Advanced Metrics

Sharpe Ratio

Return per unit of risk.

Tip

Above 1 is good, above 2 is excellent.

Average Win / Average Loss

Average size of winning vs losing trades.

Tip

Wins should be meaningfully larger than losses.

Average Holding Period

Average days each position was held.

Market Exposure (%)

How much of the time your money was actually invested.

Maximum Consecutive Losses

Longest losing streak.

Tip

Prepare emotionally for this — even good strategies have losing streaks.

Charts

Equity Curve

How your portfolio value changed over time.

Tip

Smooth rises are healthy. Sharp drops show risk events.

Drawdown Chart

How far below the previous peak you were at each point.

Tip

Deep valleys mean large temporary losses.

Monthly Returns

Performance broken down by month.

Tip

Consistency across months is usually a good sign.

Important

Past performance does not guarantee future results. Backtests are hypothetical and simplify real-world trading.
