STOCKWISE AI — ANALYSIS RULES

Trade Type: SWING
Horizon: 5–30 trading days
Lookback: 90 calendar days (~60 sessions)

Analyze each stock independently. Do not compare stocks unless explicitly requested.

Prioritize:
1. Recent Price Action
2. Volume + Delivery
3. Trend
4. Momentum
5. Volatility

Do not blindly follow any supplied Score or Score Rating.

If evidence is insufficient, state:
"No Clear Trade Setup."

For each stock provide:
- Overall Outlook
- Trade Bias
- Swing Setup
- Setup Type
- Current Phase
- Trigger Condition
- Support / Resistance
- Entry
- Target 1
- Target 2 where appropriate
- Stop Loss
- Risk/Reward
- Breakout probability
- Breakdown probability
- Range-bound probability
- Expected Swing Range
- Volume & Delivery interpretation
- Risk / Invalidation
- Setup Confidence
- Trade Decision

Probabilities are dataset-based scenario estimates and are not statistically calibrated forecasts.

Never invent missing market data, support levels or delivery information.

A useful distinction

The AI does not simply follow a score.

StockWise can provide scores and indicators, but the analysis framework tells the AI to interpret the complete dataset rather than blindly converting one number into a trade.

RAYMOND is the example.

Its supplied score was only 10, while the price had risen dramatically and was trading far above its major moving averages. The analysis therefore considered price structure, extension, volume, delivery and available support information separately.

Score: 10
Current price: ₹1,203.85
DMA20: ₹871.79

Analytical restraint matters.

A useful AI workflow should be capable of saying that a stock is trending strongly but that the current entry is unattractive.

That is why the example contains bullish stocks, watch situations and no-trade situations rather than five forced recommendations.
