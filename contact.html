What StockWise is — and isn't

StockWise is designed to act as an instant assistant for working with historical stock market data. It helps organise market information, calculate indicators and prepare structured data that can be used for further analysis, including analysis with AI tools.

StockWise does not provide personalised investment advice, stock tips, buy or sell recommendations, or guaranteed returns. The information and tools provided through StockWise are intended to help users research and analyse historical market data.

Any investment or trading decision remains the responsibility of the user. Users should independently evaluate information before making financial decisions.
