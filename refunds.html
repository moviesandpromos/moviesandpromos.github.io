Refund Policy
📅 Effective Date: September 2026

StockWise provides digital software and stock-market data services through paid subscriptions.

Refunds

Subscription payments are generally non-refundable once the subscription has been activated and the user has gained access to the paid features or data.

A refund may be considered in the following circumstances:

  • The customer was charged but the subscription was not activated due to a technical issue.
  • The customer was charged more than once for the same subscription.
  • The customer was charged an incorrect amount due to an error on our part.
  • StockWise is unable to provide the purchased service due to a technical issue attributable to StockWise and the issue cannot reasonably be resolved.

📋 How to request a refund

Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of the payment, along with the payment/order reference and details of the issue.

Refunds approved by StockWise will normally be processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. The actual time taken for the credit to appear may depend on the customer's bank or payment provider.

Cancellation

Users may cancel their subscription at any time. Cancellation will prevent renewal of the subscription, but does not automatically entitle the user to a refund for the current subscription period.

⚠️ Important

Cancellation stops future renewals. It does not trigger an automatic refund for the current billing cycle.

Trial Period

If a free trial is offered, users may use the service during the applicable trial period. No refund is applicable for a subscription purchased after or during the trial period unless the payment qualifies under the refund conditions above.

Contact

For refund or cancellation-related queries, customers may contact StockWise support through the contact details provided on the StockWise website or application.
